3am Deploy

Every redesign I ship starts with a 301 map

Relaunch without losing the traffic you've earned.

Redesigns feel like a visual project. For organic traffic they are migrations: every old URL is a contract you either honor or break.

The story in 3 steps

  1. Inventory before pixels

    Situation New URLs are cleaner and marketing wants the rebrand live.

    Decision Build the redirect map before design sign-off.

    Tradeoff A day of URL work delays mockups.

    What I choose Collect old URLs from sitemap, analytics landings, Search Console, and backlinks first.

    What happens in prod Traffic slides for weeks when launches skip the map; recovery is slower than the redirect work would have been.

    • developers.google.comSite moves with URL changes

  2. One hop to the best match

    Situation Someone suggests sending every old path to the homepage.

    Decision Map each old URL to the closest new page.

    Tradeoff Partial matches take judgment and content work.

    What I choose Server-side 301, single hop, update internal links and sitemap to the new URLs.

    What happens in prod Mass homepage redirects look like soft 404s to search engines.

    • developers.google.comSite moves with URL changes
    • developers.google.com301 redirects

  3. Keep redirects and watch crawl

    Situation The launch team wants to remove old rules after a month.

    Decision Treat redirects as part of the asset you bought with the old site.

    Tradeoff Long redirect lists are tedious to maintain.

    What I choose Keep 301s indefinitely; crawl the old list after launch; watch 404s and indexing for eight weeks.

    What happens in prod Removing redirects early erases years of links for a short-term tidy config file.

A pattern I've seen more times than I can count: new design, new CMS, cleaner URLs. Launch day goes fine. Then search traffic slides, week after week.

What breaks in prod: no redirects. Every URL Google had indexed, and every backlink the business earned over years, now returns a 404.

That's why the 301 map is the first deliverable on every redesign I ship, before any design work:

My call: the map costs a day before design starts. I put it first on every redesign, ahead of any pixels. A redesign is a migration. Treat it like one.

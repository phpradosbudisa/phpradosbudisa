The pre-launch pass that keeps launch week quiet.

20 years of shipping software, and launch day still breaks in the same places. This is the list I go through before anything goes live, mine or a client's.

What breaks in prod is rarely the feature. It's the cert nobody renews, the reset email in spam, the migration that locks a table.

Paste into your coding assistant. 
Audit this project against the 20 items below. For each: pass / fail / not applicable, the file or setting that proves it, and the smallest fix. Don't change code until I confirm.

DOMAIN & HTTPS
1. HTTPS only. Every http:// URL 301s to https://.
2. www and the bare domain resolve to ONE canonical host.
3. Certificates auto-renew, and something alerts you 14 days before expiry.
SECRETS & ACCESS
4. No secret keys in the frontend bundle. Anything that ever shipped to a browser gets rotated.
5. Database access rules deny by default. Test them logged in as a second, ordinary user.
6. Rate limits on login, signup, password reset, OTP and contact forms.
HARDENING
7. Security headers: HSTS, nosniff, Referrer-Policy, frame-ancestors, and a CSP rolled out in Report-Only first.
8. Dependency audit is clean or triaged; the lockfile is committed.
DATA
9. Automated backups, plus one real restore into a scratch environment. Time it.
10. Every migration has a rollback, or a written roll-forward plan.
MONEY & EMAIL
11. Payment webhooks: signature verified, idempotent, safe to retry. App state comes from the webhook, not the success page.
12. A refund and a declined card tested end to end.
13. SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the domain you send from.
14. Password reset and verification emails tested in Gmail AND Outlook.
SEEING PROBLEMS
15. Error tracking that alerts a human, not a dashboard nobody opens.
16. Uptime check on a real path (log in, load data), not just the homepage.
17. Logs contain no passwords, tokens or personal data.
SEARCH & ROLLBACK
18. Titles, meta descriptions and OG image per page, sitemap.xml live, robots.txt not blocking production, staging noindex removed.
19. Replacing an old site? A 301 map from every old URL.
20. Rollback in one step, and a named person on call for the first 72 hours.

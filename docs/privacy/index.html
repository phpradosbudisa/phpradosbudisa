Privacy

At launch, this site collects no personal data through forms. Reference list progress is stored only in your browser (localStorage).

Purpose & legal basis

Static episode pages for information only. No account system.

Analytics

When enabled, the site loads Google Analytics 4 with Consent Mode v2 defaults: ad_storage, analytics_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization stay denied and are never updated. No cookie banner is shown. Measurement uses cookieless pings only; this site does not set analytics cookies.

Google may receive page URL, referrer, and coarse device/browser signals. Outbound link clicks fire a custom event (outbound_click) with the destination URL and link label when analytics is enabled.

Processors

Hosting: GitHub Pages. Analytics (if enabled): Google Analytics 4.

Retention & rights

No server-side personal data at launch. You can clear localStorage in your browser. Contact the site owner for privacy requests.
